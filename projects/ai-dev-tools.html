AI-Powered Developer Tools

Tool 1

PDF bill analyser

Parses utility bills from PDF and returns a structured breakdown of charges, flagging anything anomalous compared with what you'd expect.

AI email digest bot

Connects to Gmail and summarises your inbox. It scores how important each sender is and recognises senders you've previously marked as spam.
